Overview

What a sweeping visit actually covers

Creosote builds up on the inside of a flue every time a fire burns, and past a certain thickness it becomes the leading cause of chimney fires. A sweeping visit clears that buildup from the firebox all the way to the crown, using rotating brushes sized to your specific flue.

We don't just clean and leave — every visit ends with a written condition report and photos, so you can see the difference and know if anything else needs attention.

  • ✓ Full brush sweep, firebox to crown
  • ✓ Creosote and soot removal
  • ✓ Damper and smoke shelf check
  • ✓ Before-and-after photo report

Why it matters

What a clean flue actually prevents

Fewer chimney fires

Creosote is flammable — clearing it removes the single biggest fire risk in a wood-burning flue.

Better draft

A clear flue pulls smoke up and out instead of drifting back into the room.

Longer chimney life

Regular sweeping slows the wear that eventually cracks liners and masonry.

Insurance-ready records

A written report on file is often exactly what insurers ask for after a claim.
