Relining & Repair

Cracked liner replacement and masonry repair for chimneys showing heat damage or age, so a small crack doesn't turn into a full rebuild.

Book This Service Quote on visit
Overview

What a relining and repair visit actually covers

Every crack in a flue liner or joint in the masonry is a path for heat to reach the wood framing behind the wall — and unlike a sweep or inspection, this isn't something that fixes itself with time. Left alone, a small crack widens with every heating season until the repair becomes a full rebuild.

We start every relining job with an on-site assessment so you know exactly what's needed before any work begins, and we photograph the repair at each stage so you have a record of what was done.

  • ✓ On-site structural assessment
  • ✓ Cracked liner replacement
  • ✓ Masonry and mortar joint repair
  • ✓ Photo-documented repair report

Why it matters

What a timely repair actually prevents

Stops heat transfer

A sealed liner keeps heat from reaching the wood framing behind the wall.

Restores structure

Repointed mortar joints bring the masonry back to full strength.

Avoids a full rebuild

Catching damage early is a fraction of the cost of rebuilding a chimney.

Extends chimney life

A properly relined flue can add years back to an aging chimney.
