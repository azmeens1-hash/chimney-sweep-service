Overview

What a cap installation visit actually covers

A capless chimney is an open invitation — rain seeps into the flue and weakens masonry from the inside, while birds and squirrels see an empty crown as a ready-made nest. A properly sized cap solves both problems in a single visit, and it's one of the few chimney fixes that's genuinely one-time rather than recurring.

We measure your flue on site rather than guessing from a catalog size, so the cap seals cleanly and doesn't restrict draft. Every installation is checked for fit before we consider the job done.

  • ✓ On-site flue measurement
  • ✓ Rain and downdraft protection
  • ✓ Bird and animal mesh guard
  • ✓ Secure mount, fit-checked on site

Why it matters

What a cap actually stops

Blocks rain damage

Keeps water from seeping into the flue and weakening masonry from the inside.

Stops nesting animals

A mesh guard keeps birds and squirrels from turning your flue into a nest.

Improves draft

The right cap shape actually reduces downdraft instead of restricting it.

A true one-time fix

Unlike sweeping or inspection, a well-fitted cap rarely needs revisiting for years.
