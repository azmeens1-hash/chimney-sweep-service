About WarmNest Chimney

Keeping Canada's homes safe and warm since 2011

Our story

Started with one van and a simple promise

WarmNest Chimney began in 2011 with a single technician, a used van, and a conviction that most chimney emergencies are preventable. Fifteen years later, we've swept, inspected, and capped thousands of chimneys across Canada — but the promise hasn't changed: show up on time, tell homeowners exactly what we find, and never leave a job half-explained.

Every technician on our team is trained and certified before they touch a client's hearth, and every visit ends with a written report and photos, not just a verbal "it's fine." We built the business on referrals from satisfied homeowners, and that's still where most of our new clients come from today.

Our journey

Milestones along the way

Now serving Canada and surrounding regions with the same promise we started with.
