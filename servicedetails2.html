Safety Inspection

A camera-assisted check of the liner, damper, and masonry, finished with a written condition report so you know exactly what shape your chimney is in.

Book This Service From ₹1,199
Overview

What an inspection visit actually covers

Most chimney problems don't announce themselves until they're expensive — a hairline crack in the liner, a damper that no longer seals, mortar joints starting to erode. A safety inspection catches these early, using a camera to check sections of the flue that can't be seen from the firebox.

We recommend an inspection every year, or any time you buy a home with an existing fireplace you haven't had checked. Every visit ends with a written condition report so you have a record to refer back to.

  • ✓ Camera-assisted liner check
  • ✓ Damper seal and operation test
  • ✓ Exterior masonry and mortar check
  • ✓ Written condition report with photos
How it works

Four steps, one visit

1

Book a slot

Tell us your flue type and whether this is a routine check or a pre-purchase inspection.

2

Camera check

A flexible camera runs the length of the liner, flagging cracks or blockages as it goes.

3

Test the damper

We open, close, and seal-test the damper, and check the smoke shelf for buildup.

4

Hand over the report

You get a written condition report with photos and a clear next-step recommendation.
