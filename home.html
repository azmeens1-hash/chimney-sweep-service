Fire hazard prevention

Most chimney fires give warning signs first

Creosote buildup, nesting animals, and cracked flue liners rarely announce themselves — until they do. Here's what we watch for on every visit.

Creosote buildup

A tar-like residue that ignites at high heat. A sweep every 12 months (or every 70 fires) and keeps layers from thickening.

Blocked flues

Bird and squirrel nests are the most common obstruction we clear, especially after a chimney sits capless over summer.

Cracked liners

Hairline cracks let heat reach the wood framing behind the wall. We check liner condition on every inspection, not just sweeps.

Poor draft

Smoke drifting back into the room usually means a draft problem — often solved with a damper adjustment or cap change.
